A little about me

Every wish begins with a little hope.

Life is short. Don't be afraid to dream, ask, experience, and enjoy the good things life has to offer.

I created this space to share some personal wishes and the experiences I hope to enjoy. I believe we can pursue our dreams with courage, effort, smart decisions, and a little help from generous people along the way.

This is a personal gifting project—not a registered charity. Any support is completely voluntary, and every kind gesture is appreciated. Whether a wish is fulfilled or still waiting, I'm grateful for the opportunity to dream and for the people who choose to be part of the journey.

— The Wishful Dreamer, Philippines ♡

How you can be part of the journey

If one of these wishes speaks to you, you may choose to support it voluntarily. You can also simply share this page or send a kind message. There is no expectation or obligation to give.

This is a personal wishlist, not a registered charity. Gifts are intended for the wishes described here. Any future payment option will be added only after its fees, eligibility, and withdrawal process are checked.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is The Wishful Dreamer?

A dreamer from the Philippines sharing personal wishes, interests, and experiences they'd love to enjoy.

Is this a charity or nonprofit organization?

No. Make My Wish Come True is a personal gifting project and is not presented as a registered charity.

Are visitors required to give?

Not at all. Any support is completely voluntary. Visitors are welcome to browse, share the website, or simply send encouragement.

How are wishlist prices determined?

Amounts are estimates for planning. Final prices may change depending on product selection, availability, shipping, and other costs.

How is progress updated?

Progress will only be updated when support has actually been received and verified. This demonstration website does not process payments.
