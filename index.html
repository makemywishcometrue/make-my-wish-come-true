A little about me

My Story — Make My Wish Come True

Life is short. Don't be afraid to dream, ask, experience, and enjoy the good things life has to offer.

Hello, and welcome to my little corner of the internet!

I'm someone who believes that life is meant to be experienced, not merely spent waiting for the right moment.

I created Make My Wish Come True because I want to explore a different way of living — one that embraces courage, creativity, opportunity, and the freedom to pursue the things that make life meaningful.

I have personal dreams, wishes, and experiences I'd love to achieve. Some may seem simple, while others might be a little ambitious. But I believe that good things in life are worth pursuing, especially when we approach them with effort, bravery, smart decisions, and a little creativity.

I also believe that there are generous people around the world who genuinely enjoy making others happy. Some people find joy in giving, surprising someone, or becoming part of another person's beautiful experience. And I think that's something worth celebrating.

This website is my way of putting that belief into practice.

Here, I'll share some of my personal wishes, dreams, and experiences I'd love to make possible. If you happen to find something that resonates with you and feel happy to contribute, send a gift, or support one of my dreams, I would be sincerely grateful.

I don't believe life should be all about receiving, nor should generosity be about how much someone can afford to give. Sometimes, even the smallest gesture can create genuine happiness.

I want to experience more of what life has to offer while encouraging others to discover the joy of generosity along the way.

After all, everything is temporary. Our time, opportunities, and experiences are precious. So why not make the most of the beautiful things we can experience while we're here?

I believe I deserve good things in life, and I believe you do, too.

Thank you for visiting, for reading my story, and perhaps, for becoming a little part of it.

With gratitude,

A Dreamer from the Philippines ♡

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is The Wishful Dreamer?

A dreamer from the Philippines sharing personal wishes, interests, and experiences they'd love to enjoy.

Is this a charity or nonprofit organization?

No. Make My Wish Come True is a personal gifting project and is not presented as a registered charity.

Are visitors required to give?

Not at all. Any support is completely voluntary. Visitors are welcome to browse, share the website, or simply send encouragement.

How are wishlist prices determined?

Amounts are estimates for planning. Final prices may change depending on product selection, availability, shipping, and other costs.

How is progress updated?

Progress will only be updated when support has actually been received and verified. This demonstration website does not process payments.
